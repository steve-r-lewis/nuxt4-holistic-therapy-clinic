# **Don’t You Get Tired?**

It is possibly the most common question I get asked as a massage therapist. The truth? Sometimes\! But usually, I'm more likely to feel that way after a day of decorating or digging in the garden.

While someone who doesn't practice massage might get sore hands quickly from working on someone’s shoulders, as therapists, we build up specific strength and experience over time. However, I do have to take extra care of my hands because they are quite literally the tools of my trade. It makes me a little wary of daily hazards; I hate paper cuts, sharp knives, and slippery ground—especially when you are as accident-prone as I am\!

## **A Balancing Act**

Being a massage therapist is a unique, deeply rewarding, yet physically and emotionally demanding profession. Like many roles in the caring professions, it is something you are naturally drawn to. It involves a constant balancing act between nurturing others and protecting your own body and energy.

Despite the hard work, it truly is the best job. It provides a profound sense of joy and an immediate satisfaction in seeing a client walk in limping or stressed and walk out pain-free or completely relaxed.

## **Your Health is the Priority**

Your success is our success. My goal is always to make you feel better—that’s why therapists do what they do. Your recovery is our priority because you matter to us, and because we genuinely care about you and your health.

In summary, massage is a profession that requires you to give of yourself, but when done right, it offers a level of purpose and connection that is hard to find elsewhere.

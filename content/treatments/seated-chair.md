# **Seated Chair Massage:**

An alternative to a treatment on a massage table.

## **Seated Chair Massage:**

Comfortable, upright treatment. Ideal for those with mobility issues who find traditional massage tables difficult. Choose to stay fully clothed or wear a sleeveless top for a deeper treatment using oils.

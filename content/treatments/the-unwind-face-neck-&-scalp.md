**A 60-minute face, neck and scalp massage designed to promote relaxation while easing tension where you need it most.**

The treatment begins with gentle manual lymphatic drainage, followed by massage to the neck and face using a botanical facial blend of jojoba and carefully selected wild-harvested essential oils.

The facial massage can be adapted to suit you. It may be a general, relaxing facial massage, or, where appropriate, incorporate more focused techniques to work with areas of tension associated with TMJ dysfunction, headaches, migraines or sinus congestion. This allows the treatment to remain relaxing while addressing specific areas that may need more attention.

The treatment finishes with a soothing scalp massage and a delicate botanical spritz, with a more earthy, masculine-scented blend also available. This is  to seal in the benefits of the oils.

Please arrive with a clean, make-up-free face so the treatment can begin straight away.

**60 minutes of uninterrupted time for your face, neck and scalp.**

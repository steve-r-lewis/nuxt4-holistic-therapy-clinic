A gentle, flowing full-body massage for when you need to slow down and unwind.

This is the kind of massage that asks nothing of you. Combining  long, rhythmic strokes and a pressure that creates ease and relaxation. Unlike clinical or sports massages that target deep-seated injuries or specific muscle knots, this practice focuses on the superficial layers of muscle to induce a profound state of calm.

This is not a traditional spa facial. It is a high-performance manual therapy.  

The treatment begins with manual lymphatic drainage to clear sluggish fluid from the face and neck, followed by targeted neck massage and gentle stretches. I then use myofascial techniques to release facial muscle tension, ease TMJ discomfort, relieve sinus pressure, and soften tight forehead muscles. Targeted work around the eyes reduces strain and fatigue. A soothing scalp massage completes the experience, melting headaches and shifting the nervous system into deep relaxation.
   
Each session includes a bespoke oil blend formulated with wild-harvested, therapeutic-grade essential oils selected for your skin type and their restorative properties.
Results: A refreshed, relaxed appearance with deeply nourished skin.
